Protecting Microservices with Envoy Proxy and SGNL Introduction Protected Systems are applications, services, or infrastructure that you want to protect with SGNL. In this guide, we’ll describe how to enhance access control for your Microservice infrastructure via an integration betweem SGNL and Envoy Proxy. In doing so, we’ll enable continuous, centralized access management that leverages a central set of human-readable policies.
