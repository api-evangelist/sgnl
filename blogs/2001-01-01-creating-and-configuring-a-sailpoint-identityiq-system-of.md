Creating and Configuring a SailPoint IdentityIQ System of Record SailPoint IdentityIQ provides comprehensive identity governance and administration (IGA) capabilities that bring critical compliance, risk management, and identity lifecycle context into SGNL for sophisticated access control decisions. By integrating IdentityIQ with SGNL, you gain deep visibility into user identities, role assignments, entitlement management, policy violations, approval workflows, and the complete governance lifecycle that governs identity and access management within your organization.
