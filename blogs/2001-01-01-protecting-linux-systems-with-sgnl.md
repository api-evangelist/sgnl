Protecting Linux Systems with SGNL Introduction Protected Systems are applications, services, or infrastructure that you want to protect with SGNL. In this guide, we’ll enhance access control for your Linux Servers by integrating them with SGNL’s Pluggable Authentication Module (PAM) for Linux.
