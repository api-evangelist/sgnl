Creating and Configuring a Google Workspace System of Record Google Workspace provides comprehensive organizational identity and collaboration data that serves as a foundational element for sophisticated access control decisions. By integrating Google Workspace with SGNL, you bring essential user information, organizational structure, group memberships, and administrative context into your access control framework, enabling policies that reflect how your organization actually operates and collaborates.
