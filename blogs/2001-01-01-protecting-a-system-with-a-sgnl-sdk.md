Protecting a System with a SGNL SDK Introduction Protected Systems are applications, services, or infrastructure that you want to protect with SGNL. In this guide, we’ll explore using one of SGNL’s SDK as the enforcement point for policy decisions made in the SGNL Platform. For a complete overview of how to evaluate access in your application or service from a SGNL SDK, visit our developer documentation .
