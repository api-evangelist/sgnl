Adapters Adapters are proxy-like services that are used by SGNL to communicate to their respective Systems of Record. The figure above depicts where adapters are placed in the ingestion flow from the SoRs to the SGNL platform. Adapters communicate with SoR APIs in a protocol the SoR supports and retrieve data about resources and their attributes.
